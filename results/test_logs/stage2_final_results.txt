--- Scope ---
This is a systems/implementation report. NO comparative claim is made against prior
methods. Baseline numbers from other papers are deliberately NOT reproduced here: they
were measured in different environments, at different sample sizes, and under different
evaluation protocols, so tabulating them alongside these numbers would be misleading.

--- IMPORTANT: privileged information ---
The intention-prediction module runs with sim.predict_method = 'truth', which
forward-simulates each pedestrian's ACTUAL ORCA policy and goal to obtain their true
future trajectory. This is ground-truth simulator state that no deployed robot could
have. These results are therefore an ORACLE UPPER BOUND on what a learned predictor
could achieve, not a deployable-system result.

The occlusion module was audited separately and is clean: the frozen Label_VAE encodes
ground-truth occupancy only during training (latent distillation loss); at inference
only the Sensor_VAE, which sees the partial LiDAR grid, feeds the policy.

--- Evaluation protocol note ---
At n=500 (the common sample size in this literature) a 2 percentage-point difference in
success rate has ~19% statistical power. n=3500 with 3 training seeds was used here to
avoid drawing conclusions from differences that cannot be distinguished from noise.
